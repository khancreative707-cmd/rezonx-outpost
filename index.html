ARCHIVEDMOXIE · Apr 20, 2021
MOXIE split Martian CO2 into oxygen, the first time humans made oxygen on another planet. That is where your oxygen bonus card comes from.
ARCHIVEDX9.3 solar flare · Sep 6, 2017
The largest flare of its solar cycle, logged by NOAA. Radiation arrives in minutes; shielding buys time. Your storm sol uses this event.
ARCHIVEDGlobal dust storm · June 2018
A planet-encircling dust storm cut sunlight to the Opportunity rover. Solar power is not guaranteed on Mars.
ARCHIVEDGannon storm · May 10-11, 2024
The strongest geomagnetic storm in 20 years scrambled satellites worldwide. Space weather does not care which planet you are near.
